ChatCar Daily Privacy Policy

For users in the United States and Canada · Effective date: October 23, 2026

Youngwin Co., Ltd (“Youngwin,” “we,” “us”) makes ChatCar Daily (the “App”), an app that uses AI to read photos of meals, nutrition labels, receipts, and prescriptions and keeps your nutrition, medication, and spending records. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, where it is processed, and the choices and rights you have.

1. Summary

  • We collect the photos you choose to analyze, the results and records you save, and a few account details.
  • Original photos are not stored on our servers. They are deleted right after analysis.
  • We do not sell your personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or use advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Your records are stored on our servers in South Korea. Some processing is done by service providers in the United States.
  • You can delete individual records or your entire account in the App at any time.

Information created when you use the App

  • Analysis results and records — the items, amounts, nutrients, and medication details extracted from your photos, and the records you save.
  • Account identifier — a random identifier assigned when you first open the App, including when you use it as a guest. Your records are linked to this identifier.
  • Usage counts — how many AI scans you have used, to apply free-trial and subscription limits.
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active subscription and when it renews, received from Apple or Google through our subscription provider. We never receive your payment card details.
  • App settings and technical data — your App language, region, and time zone setting; App version; and server logs that include your IP address and request times, used for security and troubleshooting.

Information we do not collect

  • Face ID / fingerprint data — App Lock uses your device’s built-in authentication. Biometric data stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

3. How we use information

  • To read your photos (text recognition) and provide AI analysis.
  • To save your records and show history, statistics, and insights.
  • To apply free-trial and subscription limits and restore your subscription.
  • To back up and restore your records when you link an account.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To respond to your requests and meet legal obligations.

We use your health information only to provide the features you request. We do not use it for advertising or sell it.

We do not use your photos or records to train AI models. Our AI providers (Google Cloud Document AI and the paid Gemini API) process them only to return results to us and do not use them to train or improve their models.

5. How long we keep information

  • Original photos — deleted immediately after analysis. They are not stored on our servers.
  • Records and account information — kept until you delete them or delete your account. This applies to guest use too.
  • Server logs — kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting, then deleted.

6. Your choices and rights

  • View and delete records in the App at any time.
  • Delete your account: Account & Security → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all records stored on our servers. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.
  • Requests by email: to access, correct, delete, or get a copy of your information, or to withdraw consent, email ywapp@youngwin.co.kr. If you no longer have the App, include the email address used for your account. We will verify your request and respond within the time required by law.
  • Non-discrimination: we won’t treat you differently for exercising your rights.

U.S. state privacy rights (for example, California, Washington, Nevada)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know, access, correct, delete, and obtain a copy of your personal information, and to appeal our decision on your request. We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy (Washington and Nevada)

  • Categories collected: health goals you select; nutrition records from food photos and labels; medication information from prescriptions and labels.
  • Sources: you, through the photos and choices you make in the App.
  • Purpose: only to provide the analysis, records, and insights you request.
  • Sharing: only with the service providers in Section 4, which process it for us. We never sell consumer health data.
  • Your rights: confirm whether we collect your consumer health data, access it, delete it, and withdraw consent. Use the in-app deletion options or email us.

Canada

We handle personal information in line with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, for Québec residents, the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (as amended by Law 25). You may ask to access or correct your information, withdraw consent, and, in Québec, request data portability. If you aren’t satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Québec, the Commission d’accès à l’information.

8. Security

  • All communication with our servers is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Original photos are not stored after analysis.
  • Access to systems that hold personal information is limited to authorized staff.
  • If a security breach affects your information, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, or under 14 in Québec, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make significant changes, we will let you know in the App or on this page before they take effect.
